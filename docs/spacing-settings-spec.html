Acquiring the first few times you meet a card, before it has a rating

How often it comes upi Counting from the first time you ever see it
You're setting how many times a new card appears on each day. A day set to 0 means it doesn't come up that day at all. Say you meet a new chord on a Monday. You'd see it twice that Monday, once Tuesday, once Thursday, and once Saturday. That's five answers — and on the fifth one it gets its first accuracy rating.

If you don't practise on Thursday, nothing is lost. It waits, and shows up the next time you sit down. The one after that comes two days later — or the next time you practise, if that's longer.

Maintaining once it has a rating

First time it comes backi The very first wait after it graduates, before the rating caps it

Your rating decides the rest. Every time you get it right, the wait is multiplied — but it can never go past the ceiling for that rating. Get a card wrong and it drops back to the start. So a chord you're Fluent on drifts out to a month, while one you're Needs work on stays on your plate every day or two until it improves.

Stale what happens when you don't come back

Grace after it's duei You're late, but it isn't stale yet
7 days
from Ear Training
Grace counts from the day it was due — not from how long the wait was. A chord on a 60-day wait that came due on the 1st is late on the 2nd, and stale on the 8th. Stale doesn't change its rating and doesn't change its colour. It moves to the front of the queue and its freshness line is nearly empty.
