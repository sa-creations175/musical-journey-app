Walk it in this order

  1. Type a name for the movement at the top, and a description of how you think about it. Both are yours; the app never fills them in. The first chord opens already selected.
  2. Press Play. Watch each chord light up. Press Stop mid-way.
  3. Change Key from C to D♭ and play again. Sound moves. Flip Spelling between ♭ and ♯: G♭ becomes F♯, the sound does not change.
  4. Turn Repeat on and let it run.
  5. While it loops, switch Bass between Forward and Even.
  6. Click the E7. Press Hear this chord. Change a note, hear it again.
  7. Click the dashed D/F♯. It has no pressed notes; the app is filling the voicing in. Press notes into it and the dashed marking goes away.
  8. Set Key to No key and press Play.
  9. Open the first C, press Copy voicing. Open the second C, press Paste voicing. Each key you click in the editor sounds as you press it.
