Modal Improvisation

Pick a key and a chord the band is sitting on. The card asks which notes fit over it; press Hear It to hear the answer as a small phrase: home with a run, the chord with a run from the answer scale, where it lands with its own scale, and home again with its scale.

Key
The band is on

Answer:

Runs are the answer scale played up from the chord's root, one octave. In-key chords have no borrowed pool, so their phrase is shorter: home with a run, the chord with a run, home with a run.
